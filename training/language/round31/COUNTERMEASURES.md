# Countermeasure evidence during the frozen suffix-cleanup trial

The actual R31 learning condition remains only dedup-policy.json. The following are supplemental read-only diagnoses and future hypotheses, not additional training variants.

The fixed16 true TRAIN prefix-substitution probe finds Round25 next32 token NLL2.44145 with original context versus3.48978 with same-genre other-source context; Round30 is almost unchanged. This rejects a strong claim that these models globally ignore the prefix on this sample. Artificial32-token joins disrupt lexical and syntactic continuity, so the effect does not establish discourse understanding, unseen generalization or a benefit from contrastive learning.

Historical Round28(.30 human mixture) and29(.10) were evaluated on the already frozen8 original mid-dialogue DEV prefixes, with the same192-token greedy decoding. All16 full outputs were read: each model whole meaning0/8, long loops8/8, EOS0/8, and Python/JavaScript full8/8 agreement. Initial acknowledgements may be appropriate, but repeated acknowledgements do not advance a complete conversation. Style learning and teacher-forced NLL improvement did not establish sustained meaning. These inputs contain no artificial role labels, so those labels alone cannot explain the failure. These are exposed DEV, not new TEST and not R31 model selection.

A future boundary-framing trial is worth considering after the R31 outcome. The earlier after-newline EOS proposal risks training an empty next output at the frozen newline-ending prefixes. Before-separator EOS at true original utterance boundaries could instead teach end-of-utterance closure, but it remains untrained and introduces BPE piece splits, target/window changes and inference serialization mismatch. It must be planned as its own declared condition, audited against true source utterance offsets rather than every embedded newline. The Round29 corpus study found21 embedded original newlines that are not turn boundaries. Its audited pre-separator piece-splitting footprint is much larger than the after-separator alternative; counts must be recomputed on the actually chosen data. Do not call this an isolated change to one EOS logit or quietly pair it with another mixture, LR or model change.

If a later study uses the rejected Round29 mixture solely to isolate framing, identify Round29 as the scientific comparator and keep adopted Round25 as the separate adoption anchor. Such exploration is not adoption of Round29. Keep original canonical DEV and strict17 gates, report serialized-input differences explicitly, never count empty output as semantic success, and distinguish a short generic acknowledgement from sustained useful conversation. No framing variant has been trained here, and current results do not prove it will solve the problem.

Do not immediately expand the model or add arbitrary updates. R27 FFN growth did not improve measured meaning, while R28/29 corpus byte exposure leaves undertraining plausible but unproven as the cause of acknowledgement loops. LR has not been independently ablated in these trials. None of the current diagnoses proves a unique cause; future choices must follow the final R31 raw-generation result, not its interim scalar NLL alone.
