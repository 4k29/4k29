# Current factor and conditional future hypotheses

R32 is only the negative-scoring dropout-mode study. The fixed10,000 updates,64-token history,0.1 coefficient, positive CE dropout, data, source folds, genre/view masses, parent, optimizer, LR and gates do not change based on interim results. A nearly equal first500 DEV NLL is not grounds to declare success or change the condition.

R31 suffix cleanup slightly improved common NLL but worsened first-sentence/nonloop and failed all whole meaning. Existing original17/daily12/native8 numerical agreement means that particular failure is not supported as a JavaScript-only or export-rounding-only error. R27 FFN growth and R28/R29 human-mixture studies did not establish meaning improvement; do not immediately enlarge or blindly train more.

The new fixed8 exposed-human-DEV likelihood diagnostic finds lower average NLL under true context, with mixed individual wins. That weakens the strongest claim of globally absent conversational conditioning on these8, but artificial joins and tokenization/topic/length effects prevent a discourse-understanding claim. R29 lower human likelihood together with failed free generation reinforces the need for raw whole-output assessment.

If R32 fails, a future representation study could compare the rejected R29 human10% corpus condition against the same corpus with one original human utterance as target and its unchanged preceding raw utterances as masked context, ending only that target with EOS. Do not insert EOS throughout the input context: that would mismatch unchanged raw inference prefixes, the unresolved problem in the earlier before-newline proposal. This proposed context/one-turn target packing must be a separate round with R29 as scientific comparator and R25 as adoption anchor. It is not trained or adopted here.

Before such a future run, independently verify source utterance offsets (not arbitrary newlines), no empty targets, response targets exactly once per view, honest duplicated-context accounting, frozen speaker/source folds, same view/genre marginal masses and exact unchanged nonhuman bytes/DEV. Record effects of BPE boundary splits/random cut alignment, context masks, window and sampling changes; these are inherent representation consequences, not an EOS-logit-only intervention. No new role templates, generated positive answers or response editing. Original human language modeling, not curated instruction targets. Current observations do not prove this would solve repetition or semantics. Do not simultaneously alter mixture, LR, model or objective.

LR remains a separate unablated possibility. Limited learned context use, data exposure, token granularity, clipping effects and model capacity are not excluded. Fixed native8 prefixes plus192 generated tokens fit context256, so literal history cropping alone cannot explain those failures. None of these hypotheses replaces actual full-output measurement or relaxes the strict original language gate.

Read-only actual sampler replay of both historical10000-update human-corpus studies exactly matches their final private RNG and all target/token byte counters. Human source corpus is18,683,972 raw bytes in8,975 documents. R29 target-byte union across views covers25.4526%; R28 covers58.1692%. Cumulative human target-byte exposure is distinct from coverage and semantic diversity. Context-only exposure is not counted as a target, so this does not mean all remaining bytes were absent as inputs. This quantitatively supports underexposure as a plausible hypothesis and could justify a separately frozen continuation-dose study from an aligned historical checkpoint, but does not show that more updates will fix meaning or loops. Any such study must be separate from R32, keep LR/data/objective fixed, distinguish scientific comparator from adoptedR25, preserve actual inherited/added optimizer counts, and assess full generation.
