## Limitations

Raw acquisition and unresolved text remain private/quarantined. No sealedTEST is opened. Official pre-2022 provenance is not a mathematical human-authorship certification; current municipal prose is not independently certified human-only either. One assistant's reading is not independent native-speaker evaluation. This is a bounded exploratory dataset, not a claim that every fact has been independently checked or that broad Japanese dialogue capability has been attained.
