Wikipedia-derived contexts remain subject to original attribution and quotation checks. Dataset authors label questions/answers as human annotated. No pretrained baseline weights or tokenizer were obtained.

Only30 verbatim TRAIN contexts and their existing human QA are sampled, by the SHA selection in sample.json. No sentence repair, no generated answers, no DEV/TEST fetch, no training admission.
