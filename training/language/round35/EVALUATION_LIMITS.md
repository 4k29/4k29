# Evaluation limits

Daily conversational coherence is the intended capability. Existing original17, daily12, native8, source5 and humanDEV99 are exposed development diagnostics, not new TEST. The unchanged common-DEV checkpoint selector excludes humanDEV99. It can select a state that is worse for conversation; selected and terminal generations must therefore both be saved and assessed separately, without changing the primary selector after seeing outputs.

Auxiliary EOS applies only where an authentic human TRAIN utterance-end original target is exactly standalone newline16 and was originally unmasked. Tokens that combine newline with other bytes are excluded. Thus the eligible share differs across normal/Glyph/random-cut views. It is not training EOS at every human turn or at generic punctuation. Original newline CE remains, deliberately creating competing newline/EOS supervision. The fixed coefficient is1.0, normalized by original valid target count, not eligible count. This trial cannot isolate an EOS effect from its natural downstream effect on self-generated negatives.

Early EOS, fewer repeated tokens, lower CE, valid UTF-8 or a natural first sentence does not establish a relevant, meaningful full continuation. Empty, evasive, off-topic and internally inconsistent answers fail full meaning. Minor context-limit truncation, coherent fiction and unfamiliar names follow the original established rubric. Ambiguous interpretations and strict gate failures must be reported explicitly, without relaxing thresholds or repairing outputs.

Periodic training-loss events are every100 updates and coincide with the every10 sequence-negative updates. They are not an all-update mean. Validation remains pure original CE without either unlikelihood term or EOS auxiliary. One seed and correlated checkpoints do not provide independent statistical replications. The TRAIN99 and DEV99 samples differ in context lengths/topics; one exact duplicate pair exists within humanDEV99. Source5 are one cast family, not five independent groups.

No fresh independent generalization claim can be made from these repeatedly exposed prompts. No new TEST or reference-adapted model selection is authorized by this experiment. Historical accidental TEST-reference exposure recorded in prior rounds remains a limitation; this round does not read TEST reference paragraphs or generate new TEST answers.
